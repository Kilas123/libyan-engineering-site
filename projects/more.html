More Projects

Additional work from LEO's archive. Over six decades of practice, the firm has delivered a broad range of urban planning and residential developments across Libya — many without photographic documentation. Selected works are listed below.

Urban Planning & Residential

Khaleej Al Bumbah Residential Village

A complete residential village of 445 housing units, designed to serve a growing coastal community. Includes housing, infrastructure, and public amenities as a single planned development.

Fawakhir Tribes Resettlement Village

A residential village in the Fawakhir Tribes area, designed for the resettlement of nomadic populations in Wadi Al Bab — providing permanent housing and community infrastructure.

Benghazi 7,000-Unit Housing Project

A major residential development of 7,000 housing units in Benghazi — one of the largest housing programmes undertaken by the firm.

Benghazi 5,000-Unit Housing Project

A 5,000-unit residential project in Benghazi, forming part of the wider 60,000-unit national housing programme. Covers master planning, housing design, and site infrastructure.

Wadi Al Majinin Supervision

Construction supervision of the 5,000 housing unit project in Wadi Al Majinin — overseeing quality, schedule, and cost through to completion.

Tripoli 444-Unit Master Plan

Design of the master plan for a 444-unit residential development in Tripoli — including site layout, circulation, and open space strategy across the whole development.

South Shahhat Gardens

A sustainable residential project of 1,500 housing units, designed with an emphasis on landscape integration and environmentally considered planning.
