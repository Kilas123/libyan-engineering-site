Commercial

Aljomhuriya Bank

A proposed bank headquarters in Tripoli — a visionary architectural endeavour poised to redefine its urban landscape. A beacon of modern Islamic-inspired design, the building breaks from conventional banking structures to establish itself as a landmark within its context. Islamic-inspired screens envelop the façades, not merely as decorative elements but as functional shields against the harsh glare of sunlight, ensuring optimal interior comfort. This symbiosis of form and function gives the structure a distinctive aesthetic while underscoring its commitment to sustainable design. Mixed-Use

Gwarsha Mixed-Use Development

Located in the Al-Gwarsha area of Benghazi, on a site of strategic value in terms of both scale and development potential. The master plan defines the site boundaries, the area designated for the project's full development, and the smaller parcel owned by the client within the overall framework — providing flexibility for development planning while maintaining a clear relationship between private ownership and the broader vision.

The site benefits from frontage on two streets — a primary arterial road and a secondary local road. This dual access enhances accessibility, improves circulation efficiency, and strengthens connectivity between the project's components and their urban context, creating opportunities for efficient distribution of entrances and service movement.
